Freelance Services Agreement No. 12/SMM (sample)

Reviewed September 23, 2026 · uploaded file: services-agreement-sample.docx
Medium risk

A workable but one-sided agreement for freelance social media work: the late penalty and the right to terminate favor only the client, and there is no clear payment date. Worth discussing 3 points before signing; all three are about negotiating position, and one may also conflict with NYC freelance rules.

Risks 3

Uncapped penalty for the contractor only

high

§5.3: the contractor pays 1% of the total contract price for every day of delay, with no upper limit. Nothing applies if the client pays late. Risk for: the contractor.

"For each day of delay, Contractor shall pay Client liquidated damages equal to 1% of the total Fees under this Agreement."
Why it's a risk: after about three months of delay the penalty equals the whole fee. "Liquidated damages" (a fixed amount agreed in advance instead of proving actual losses) that look like a punishment may not be enforced in New York, but getting a court to say so takes time and money; it isn't automatic protection.

No payment date

medium ⚖️ may conflict with NY law

§4.1 says the client pays "upon satisfactory completion" but sets no date and doesn't say who decides what "satisfactory" means. Risk for: the contractor.

"Client shall pay the Fees upon satisfactory completion of the Services."
Why it matters: NYC and NYS freelance laws require a written contract with a payment date for work of $800 or more; if no date is set, payment is generally due within 30 days of completion. A question for a lawyer: does this clause meet that requirement?

Only the client can terminate freely

medium

§9.2: the client may terminate at any time without a reason; the contractor only after 30 days' written notice and only for a "material breach" (a serious violation) by the client. Risk for: the contractor.

Errors and inconsistencies 1

Reference to a missing exhibit

check

§3.2 refers to "the acceptance procedure set forth in Exhibit B", but the package only includes Exhibit A (rate sheet). There is no Exhibit B, so the acceptance procedure isn't described anywhere.

How to make it better 3

Cap the contractor's penalty

In §5.3, after "1% of the total Fees under this Agreement", add: ", provided that the aggregate amount shall not exceed 10% of the total Fees".

Set a payment date

Replace §4.1 with: "Client shall pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt. Services are deemed accepted unless Client provides written objections within 5 business days of delivery."

Make termination mutual

In §9.2, replace "Client may" with "Either Party may", or add a matching right for the contractor to terminate on the same 30 days' notice, with payment for work done up to that date.
